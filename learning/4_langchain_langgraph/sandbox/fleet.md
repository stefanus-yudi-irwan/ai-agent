# Fleet Vehicle Comparison: Tesla Model Y vs Ford Mustang Mach-E

## Tesla Model Y
- Range: 303 to 321 miles (up to 391 miles for highest range models)
- Charging: Fast charging adds ~169 miles in ~30 minutes
- Reliability: Highly positive, advanced technology, durable
- Cost: Competitive pricing, low maintenance costs
- Suitability: Spacious, safe, and technologically advanced, ideal for sales fleet

## Ford Mustang Mach-E
- Range: Up to 615 km (~382 miles)
- Charging: Around 7.8 hours with standard charger
- Performance: Up to 487 hp, rapid acceleration, strong torque
- Reliability: Well-regarded, suitable for long-distance travel
- Cost: Competitive, moderate maintenance costs

## Recommendation

Both vehicles are strong candidates for our sales fleet. The Tesla Model Y offers slightly higher range and advanced features, making it excellent for quick turnaround and tech-savvy drivers. The Ford Mustang Mach-E provides comparable range, excellent performance, and is a solid alternative for long-distance travel needs.

Given our focus on reliability, range, and charging efficiency, the Tesla Model Y is recommended for its superior range and charging capabilities, which are critical for high-volume fleet operations.
